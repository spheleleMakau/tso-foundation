TSO Foundation Empowering communities through education, sport and care

TSO Foundation is a registered Non-Profit Organisation committed to empowering young people through education, sports, skills development and community-led initiatives.

Working alongside schools, businesses, government departments and community organisations, we create opportunities that help young people reach their full potential while building safer communities.

7+ Community Partners

100% Committed to Community Development

Our Focus Areas

Programs designed to support every child and young person.

📚

Education Support

Helping learners succeed through school supplies, academic support and back-to-school programmes.

⚽

Sports Development

Supporting youth through football programmes, equipment donations, coaching and talent development.

🌱

Youth Skills Development

Helping young people gain practical life skills through workshops, leadership and community engagement.

🤝

Community Development

Building stronger communities through outreach, partnerships and social development initiatives.

Drug Awareness

Working with schools and SAPS to educate young people about substance abuse and healthy choices.

GBV Awareness

Encouraging boys and young men to become positive role models and prevent gender-based violence.
